# Code of conduct

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussion spaces. Treat people respectfully, criticize technical decisions rather than people, and allow mistakes to be corrected. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention, disclosure of private information and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive material, restrict participation or exclude a participant. They should consider severity and recurrence, protect reporter privacy and explain decisions to affected people where safe. Maintainers follow the same expectations.

Send confidential conduct reports to [alvit.work@gmail.com](mailto:alvit.work@gmail.com). Do not post private incident details or personal information in public issues or discussions. The primary maintainer identified in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) handles reports; there is currently no independent moderator or appeals panel. Affected participants may request reconsideration with relevant new information. Vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
